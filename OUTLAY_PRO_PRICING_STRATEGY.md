# Outlay Pro Pricing Strategy

**Date**: 2026-09-26  
**Market**: Pakistan (Primary)  
**Competitor Analysis**: Splitwise, Tricount, Settle Up, Batwa, HisabDost

---

## Executive Summary

Outlay Pro will launch with **3 tiers: Free → Pro → Max** to capture different market segments while maximizing revenue from power users (families, teams). The key differentiator is **family accounts with expense splits** — a gap no competitor fills.

---

## Pricing Tiers

### 1. FREE (Outlay Free)
**Target**: New users, light trackers

#### Features
- ✅ Single account manual entry
- ✅ **Event-based expense splits** (trip, dinner, etc.) — **HOOK FEATURE**
- ✅ Basic categorization
- ✅ Transaction history
- ✅ Simple reports

#### Limitations
- ❌ No SMS auto-logging
- ❌ No bank statement import
- ❌ No multi-account
- ❌ Limited to 1 event at a time (or max 3 active events)
- ❌ Ads

#### Goal
- Prove value to users
- Lock in with splits (psychological commitment)
- Drive viral growth (friend invites, settling up with others)

---

### 2. PRO (₨299/month)
**Target**: Individuals, couples, small households, freelancers

#### Features (Everything in Free +)
- ✅ **SMS Auto-Logging** (HBL, UBL, MCB, Meezan, Faysal, JazzCash, EasyPaisa)
- ✅ **Bank Statement Import** (PDF/CSV for all major PK banks)
- ✅ **Screenshot OCR** (ML Kit) for historical JazzCash/EasyPaisa data
- ✅ **Multi-Account Support** (up to 3 accounts)
- ✅ **Recurring Transaction Detection** (bills, subscriptions, salary)
- ✅ **Tax Export** (CSV/PDF for accountants)
- ✅ Unlimited event splits
- ✅ No ads

#### Why ₨299?
- Undercuts competitors (Splitwise $4.99, Money Lover $2.99)
- Affordable for Pakistani market (~₨50-100K household income)
- 3-5% conversion from free tier realistic

---

### 3. MAX (₨599/month)
**Target**: Families, roommate groups, small teams, freelancers with team

#### Features (Everything in Pro +)
- ✅ **Family Accounts** (5-10 multi-user members)
  - Shared family budget
  - Role-based access (admin, member, viewer)
  - Real-time sync across devices
  - Invite family members via email/SMS
- ✅ **Unlimited Accounts** (no 3-account limit)
- ✅ **Influencer Revenue Share Dashboard** (unique monetization angle)
  - See which influencers drove sales
  - Track revenue attribution
- ✅ Priority support (24-hour response)
- ✅ Advanced analytics & insights

#### Why ₨599?
- Multi-user infrastructure costs (real-time sync, RLS policies)
- Higher lifetime value — families/teams stick longer
- Affordable upgrade from Pro
- Unique positioning vs competitors

## Revenue Model

### Free Tier
- Ad-supported
- Optional micro-transaction: "Remove ads" (₨99/mo)

### Pro Tier
- Recurring subscription ₨299/month
- 7-day free trial

### Max Tier
- Recurring subscription ₨599/month
- 14-day free trial
- Annual discount option (₨5,990/year = 17% off)
