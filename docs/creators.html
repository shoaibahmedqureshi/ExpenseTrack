Outlay Creator Program

Outlay is a fast, private expense tracker. If you make content about money, budgeting or productivity, you can earn from the people you bring.

How it works

  • You get a personal discount code to share with your audience.
  • You earn 20% of what each subscriber who uses your code pays, paid once per subscriber.
  • Apply with your email. We review each application by hand and reply to you personally.
  • Once approved, you can sign in any time to see your code, your subscribers and what you have earned.
