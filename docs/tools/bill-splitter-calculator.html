How tax and tip are calculated

Enter the bill subtotal before tax. Tax and tip are both calculated on that subtotal (the standard convention — tip isn't charged on tax), then added together to get the total, which is divided evenly by the number of people splitting it.

When to use itemized split

Switch to Itemized Split when the group didn't order equally — add each person, add each item with its price, and tick who shared it. Tax and tip are then divided proportionally based on how much each person actually ordered, not split evenly.

Keep track after you pay

Splitting the bill is the easy part — remembering who still owes you is the hard part. Outlay logs your spending automatically so shared expenses like this don't get lost in your monthly totals.
