How the tip is calculated

The tip is calculated on the full bill amount, then added to the bill to get the total. The total is then divided evenly by the number of people splitting it. For an uneven split (e.g. someone only had a drink), do the math on individual items separately and use this tool just for the shared portion.

Keep track after you pay

Splitting the bill is the easy part — remembering who still owes you is the hard part. Outlay logs your spending automatically so shared expenses like this don't get lost in your monthly totals.
