Delete Your Outlay Account iOS

We respect your right to delete your account and all associated data from Outlay.

Send an email to 08bitsaqureshi@seecs.edu.pk with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Outlay account.

We will delete your account and all data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Open Email App

The button above opens your email app with a pre-filled message. Just add your registered email address in the body and send.

What gets deleted?

When you request account deletion, we remove:

  • Your profile (name, email, preferences)
  • All expenses and budgets
  • All expense categories you created
  • All backups and synced data on Supabase
  • Subscription status (if applicable)

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. After deletion, you can create a new account with the same email if you change your mind later.

Questions? Email 08bitsaqureshi@seecs.edu.pk.
