Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't my data syncing across devices?

Make sure you're signed into the same account on all devices. Sync runs every 30 seconds in the background or when you reconnect to the internet. Go to Settings → Check internet connection. If still not syncing after 5 minutes, try closing and reopening the app.

How do I export my expenses?

Open the Reports tab, select your desired date range, and tap "Export." Choose between CSV (spreadsheet) or PDF (printable report). The file will be ready to download or share.

Can I change my password?

Yes! Go to Settings → Account → Change Password. You'll receive a password reset email. Follow the link to set a new password.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Outlay → Tap "Cancel Subscription." You can cancel anytime; you'll keep Pro features until the end of your billing period.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at 08bitsaqureshi@seecs.edu.pk with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered email. We'll delete your account and all data within 30 days. See our full deletion page for more details.

Why can't I sign in?

Check that you're using the correct email and password. If you've forgotten your password, tap "Forgot Password?" on the login screen. Didn't receive the reset email? Check your spam folder.

Does the app work offline?

Yes! Outlay works completely offline. Your expenses, budgets, and categories are stored locally on your device. Changes sync to other devices and the cloud when you're connected to the internet.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest on our secure servers. We never sell or share your data with advertisers. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with details about what went wrong, including:

  • What were you doing when the bug occurred?
  • What did you expect to happen?
  • What actually happened?
  • Your iOS version and device type
