Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't my data syncing across devices?

Make sure you're signed into the same account on all devices. Sync runs in the background or when you reconnect to the internet. If it's still not syncing after a few minutes, try closing and reopening the app.

How do I export my expenses?

Open the Reports tab, select your desired date range, and tap "Export." Choose between CSV (spreadsheet) or PDF (printable report).

Can I change my password?

Go to Settings → Account → Change Password. You'll receive a password reset email — follow the link to set a new one.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

iOS: Settings app → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Outlay → Cancel Subscription.
Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Outlay → Cancel.
Either way, you keep Pro features until the end of your current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at contact@outlayapp.net with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered email. We'll delete your account and all data within 30 days. See our full deletion page for details.

Why can't I sign in?

Check that you're using the correct email and password. If you've forgotten your password, tap "Forgot Password?" on the login screen — and check your spam folder if the reset email doesn't arrive.

Does the app work offline?

Yes — expenses, budgets, and categories are stored locally on your device. Changes sync to other devices and the cloud once you're back online.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never sell or share it with advertisers. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with what you were doing, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and your OS version and device type. Send to contact@outlayapp.net.
