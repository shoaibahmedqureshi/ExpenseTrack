# MAX: features and pricing options (draft for a decision)

Market read from the competitor check (see the chat summary; figures come from third-party review sites, so verify on each
company's own pricing page before finalising):

- Sharing is usually bundled into the one paid tier (YNAB: 6 people for $109/yr; Monarch: partner included at $99.99/yr;
  Spendee: shared wallets already in the $14.99/yr tier) or is a modest step up (Wallet: family plan for 5 people at about 1.5x
  the individual price).
- Mid-market shared tiers cost about $4-6 a month or $35-50 a year.
- Splitwise gates free users to a few entries a day; unlimited free groups are a differentiator for Outlay.

## What MAX includes

Already built:
- Everything in Pro (unlimited scans, advanced reports, priority sync)
- Create a family and invite up to 10 people
- Shared expenses inside the family (equal / by amount / by percentage, settle up, notifications)

Being built now (this is what justifies a higher price than Pro):
- **Household budget:** the family admin sets one monthly budget (overall and per category). Members who opt in share
  their monthly *totals per category* (never individual expenses); everyone sees how the household is tracking.

Free for everyone, so not part of MAX: split groups with friends (up to 20 people), invitations by email.
